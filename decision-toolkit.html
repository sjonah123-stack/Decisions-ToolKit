Model:

Sort tasks by urgency and importance. Important+Urgent → do now. Important+Not urgent → schedule. Urgent+Not important → delegate. Neither → drop.

Weigh the pros and cons of each option (rate each 1–5). Net = total pro weight − total con weight. Best for: a yes/no call or a two-option choice where reasoning matters.

For decisions under uncertainty. Give each option its possible outcomes with a probability and a value (money or a 0–100 score). Expected value = Σ (probability × value). Best for: bets, gambles, go/no-go calls with real downside.

Compare options financially over a time horizon. Net = (annual benefit − annual cost) × years − upfront. ROI = net ÷ total cost. Payback = upfront ÷ annual net. Best for: "is it worth it?" investments and build-vs-buy.
